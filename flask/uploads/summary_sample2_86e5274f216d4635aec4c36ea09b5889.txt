Social media has a significant influence on student learning, with debates on its benefits and drawbacks. While some view it as a distraction, others see it as a valuable learning tool. The key lies in how students manage their time and usage. Social media offers educational content and collaboration opportunities, but excessive use can harm concentration and study habits. Strategic use, like setting time limits and following educational accounts, can help students leverage social media for learning. Educators can also teach digital literacy to promote responsible online behavior. Ultimately, the impact of social media on learning depends on how students use it, balancing its potential benefits with potential distractions.
