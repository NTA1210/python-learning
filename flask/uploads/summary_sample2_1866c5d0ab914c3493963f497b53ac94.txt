Social media's influence on student learning is a widely debated topic. While some view it as a distraction that hampers academic performance, others see it as a valuable educational tool. The key lies in how students manage their time and usage. Social media can provide access to educational content, facilitate collaboration, and enhance critical thinking skills. However, excessive use can lead to distractions, reduced study habits, and stress from comparisons. The solution is strategic use, involving setting limits, following educational accounts, and participating in academic groups. With proper management and digital literacy skills, social media can be a beneficial learning tool for students.
