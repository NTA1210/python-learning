Social media's impact on student learning is a widely debated topic. While some view it as a distraction that hinders academic performance, others see it as a valuable learning tool when used intentionally. Proper time management and usage are key factors in determining social media's influence. Platforms like YouTube and online study groups offer educational content and collaboration opportunities, enhancing critical thinking skills. However, excessive use of platforms like Instagram and TikTok can lead to distractions and impact study habits negatively. By using social media strategically, setting time limits, and engaging in educational discussions, students can leverage its benefits for learning. Educators can also help by teaching digital literacy skills. Ultimately, the impact of social media on learning depends on how it is used, with the potential to enhance learning or hinder academic success.
