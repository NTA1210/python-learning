Social media's impact on student learning is a topic of debate among educators and researchers. While some view it as a distraction, others see it as a valuable learning tool if used intentionally. Social media can provide access to educational content, facilitate collaboration, and help develop critical thinking skills. However, excessive use of platforms like Instagram and TikTok can negatively affect concentration and study habits. The key lies in strategic usage, setting time limits, and following educational accounts. With proper management, social media can enhance learning by fostering collaboration and skill development while avoiding distractions that hinder academic success.
