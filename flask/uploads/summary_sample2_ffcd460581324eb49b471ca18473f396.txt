Social media's impact on student learning is a subject of debate among educators and researchers. While some view it as a distraction that hampers academic performance, others see it as a valuable educational tool if used intentionally. Social media can provide access to educational content, facilitate collaboration, and help develop critical thinking skills. However, excessive use of platforms like Instagram and TikTok can lead to decreased concentration and study habits. Strategic use, such as setting time limits and following educational accounts, can help students benefit from social media while avoiding distractions. Ultimately, the key lies in how students manage their time and usage to leverage social media for learning purposes effectively.
