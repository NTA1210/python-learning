Social media is a significant part of students' lives, with debates on its impact on learning. While some see it as a distraction, others view it as a valuable learning tool. The key lies in how students manage their time and usage. Social media offers educational content and collaboration opportunities, but excessive use can hinder academic performance. Strategic use, like setting time limits and following educational accounts, can enhance learning. Educators can help by teaching digital literacy skills. Ultimately, the impact of social media on learning depends on users' habits and intentions.
